## Domain — Fishing

The established Fishing watch remains active. Its authoritative discovery framework, Worker role, Shimano/Fenwick/G. Loomis rod targets, Stradic FM reel targets, verification priorities, and price anchors remain as previously defined.

### Fishing core rules
- General rod REQUIRED: Medium-Light or Medium power; Fast or Extra Fast action.
- General rod PREFERRED: 6'5"–7'2"; 2-piece.
- Worker: go-to light spinning role; ML/F strongly preferred; 6'8"–7'2"; 2-piece strongly preferred; shorter rear handle and continuous/full cork preferred; roughly 1/8 oz through 3/8–5/8 oz useful envelope.
- Worker value baseline: Fenwick Eagle Walleye `EGLW70ML-FS-2`, about $99.95 new.
- Worker performance benchmark: Fenwick HMG Walleye `HMGW72ML-FS-2`, about $179.95 new.
- Priority Shimano rods: Expride, Zodias, Cumara, Crucial, Poison Adrena.
- Priority G. Loomis families: GLX, IMX, IMX-Pro, NRX and comparable older/discontinued premium models.
- Priority Fenwick discovery: Eagle, HMG, Elite, Walleye, Inshore and older/discontinued Worker-compatible models.
- Priority reels: Shimano Stradic FM `ST1000HGFM` and 2500-size, especially `ST2500HGFM`.

## Domain — Hat:Machine

### Mission question
For every candidate machine, answer first:

> **Can this machine sew a leather sweatband into a felt hat?**

This is an application-specific machinery watch, not a generic sewing-machine search. A machine's ability to sew leather alone does not qualify it. The machine must have geometry, feed/stitch behavior, controllability, and practical access appropriate for attaching a leather sweatband around the inside circumference of a formed felt hat crown without requiring destructive flattening or impractical manipulation of the hat.

### Candidate classes
Search broadly across:
- purpose-built hat or millinery sweatband sewing machines;
- vintage industrial machines explicitly designed for hat sweatband insertion/attachment;
- cylinder-bed, post-bed, off-the-arm, free-arm, curved-needle, or other specialty industrial machines whose geometry may genuinely suit the operation;
- older/discontinued industrial machines that sellers may poorly identify;
- complete tables/stands/motors and machine heads when acquisition value is compelling.

Do not assume a machine qualifies merely because it is described as industrial, leather-capable, cylinder-bed, post-bed, cobbler, patcher, or heavy-duty.

### Evaluation states
Classify the mission fit as:
- **YES — PURPOSE-BUILT:** designed specifically for hat/sweatband work or strongly documented for this exact operation.
- **YES — PRACTICAL:** not purpose-built, but geometry and stitch/feed behavior make leather-sweatband installation in a formed felt hat realistically workable.
- **MAYBE / VERIFY:** potentially suitable, but critical geometry, feed, stitch, presser-foot, clearance, or application evidence is missing.
- **NO:** cannot practically perform the operation or is dedicated to another operation.

### Required evaluation dimensions
For each promising listing assess:
- exact maker/model and likely generation;
- machine class and intended original operation;
- bed/arm geometry and whether a formed hat can rotate around the sewing point;
- throat/cylinder/post/free-arm dimensions when available;
- stitch type and suitability for sweatband attachment;
- feed mechanism and ability to control leather + felt without marking, stretching, or distorting either;
- presser-foot/needle arrangement and available edge control/guides;
- speed control, especially whether slow controlled sewing is practical;
- leather capability at sweatband thickness, while recognizing that leather capability alone is insufficient;
- availability of needles, feet, guides, cams, loopers, bobbins, belts, motors, and service parts;
- completeness: head only vs table/stand/motor/pedal/attachments;
- operational condition and restoration risk;
- asking price, delivered/acquisition cost, realistic market value, and transaction risk.

### Search terms and discovery behavior
Use both exact-purpose and broad sleeper searches. Useful query concepts include:
- hat sweatband sewing machine
- sweatband machine
- hat band sewing machine
- millinery sewing machine
- hat sewing machine industrial
- curved needle hat machine
- cylinder bed sewing machine
- post bed sewing machine
- off the arm sewing machine
- free arm industrial sewing machine
- vintage industrial sewing machine hat
- fur felt hat sewing machine

Search photos and model plates when seller titles are vague. Poorly identified vintage industrial equipment may be especially valuable if its geometry reveals the correct application.

### Explicit negative example
**Juki MB-372 / Z002 button sewer:** mission fit **NO**. It is a dedicated button-sewing machine. Its ability to stitch material does not provide the continuous seam and work geometry required to install a leather sweatband around a felt hat.

### Deal behavior
The objective is not simply to find a machine that technically can do the job. Favor machines that combine:
- strong mission fit;
- controllable, repeatable sweatband installation;
- serviceable/common-enough consumables or parts;
- manageable restoration risk;
- reasonable footprint and power requirements;
- compelling acquisition value.

Purpose-built vintage machines can deserve attention even when obscure, but scarcity of parts/knowledge must be reflected in risk and deal score. Adapted machines should not outrank purpose-built solutions merely because they are more common or cheaper.

### Search geography
For local/used-market discovery, use target ZIP **98053** with an approximately **500-mile radius** where the source supports geographic filtering. Shipping-capable national listings may also qualify when delivered cost remains compelling.
