# Fishing Deal Watch — Watchlist

This file is the authoritative durable target list. Exact model numbers take precedence over family-level descriptions when available.

## Shimano rods

### Expride
Priority sleeper/value target. Search current and older generations, especially listings with poor identification or unusually low pricing.

### Zodias
Value-oriented premium target. Search current and prior generations where delivered price is compelling.

### Cumara
Older/discontinued Shimano target with garage-sale potential. Verify exact generation/model from markings and photos.

### Crucial
Older/discontinued Shimano target. Pay special attention to unusual lengths/powers and local listings where sellers may not know current collector/use value.

### Poison Adrena
Higher-end/JDM target. Shipping from Japan can erase apparent bargain pricing, so evaluate delivered cost carefully.

## G. Loomis rods

Search comparable premium Loomis rods when value is compelling, including:
- GLX
- IMX
- IMX-Pro
- NRX
- related older/discontinued premium Loomis rods

Do not assume Shimano-branded rods are rebadged G. Loomis blanks. Treat cross-brand comparison as value/technology context, not identity.

## Fenwick Eagle

### EGLW70ML-FS-2
Reference configuration:
- 7'0"
- Medium Light
- Fast
- 2-piece
- 1/8–5/8 oz lure
- 4–10 lb line
- reference new price: about $99.95

Deal guidance:
- Used examples generally need to be around $60 or less to be strongly interesting.
- Genuinely new examples around ~$75 can be attractive.
- A used rod near $95 delivered is normally weak when a new Eagle is around $100.

## Fenwick HMG

Prioritize HMG spinning rods broadly similar to the Eagle reference configuration:
- roughly 7'0"–7'2"
- Medium Light
- Fast
- preferably 2-piece
- around 1/8–5/8 oz lure rating

## Verification priorities

### Rods
Inspect or flag:
- exact model markings
- blank damage or repairs
- guide inserts and frames
- ferrules on multi-piece rods
- reel seat
- altered handles
- mismatched model/description

### Reels
Inspect or flag:
- exact SKU
- cosmetic wear
- spool-lip damage
- handle and bail condition
- signs of saltwater abuse
- missing box/parts
- seller feedback and return terms
